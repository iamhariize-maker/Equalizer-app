## Unmodelled loudness effects

`predictedGuideLoudnessDeltaDb` models static EQ and the stereo guide. Bass Resolve (dynamic, capped at 1.5/0.75 dB), bass unmasking (<= 2 dB, only while cutting) and the spatial residual (<= 4 dB, band-limited, signal-dependent) are not predicted statically; they are covered by rendering the live implementation in the loudness-matched blind comparison.

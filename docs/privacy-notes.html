SVAN / BETA PRIVACY NOTES

Your listening.
On your device.

An overview of the beta’s audio processing, data requests and optional sharing.

Audio stays local

Svan processes audio on your Android device. It does not upload captured playback or save it as audio files. The experimental Audiophile engine uses Android playback-capture consent and a foreground notification; permission is requested for each new session.

Svan does not intentionally capture microphone input. Android’s RECORD_AUDIO permission is required for playback capture and measurement probes.

Headphone data and local settings

When you request headphone tuning, Svan downloads AutoEq measurement, index and target files from GitHub. Those requests expose ordinary network metadata, including your IP address and requested paths, to GitHub and its delivery infrastructure.

Settings, presets, cached tuning data and calibration curves are stored locally. Curve imports and settings exports use files you explicitly choose. Exported files may identify your headphones; they contain no audio, Android permissions or signing keys.

Optional controls and detection

The floating Svaramanas bubble uses optional overlay access. In-app controls work without that access. Enhanced music detection uses a fixed Shizuku request, after your authorization, to grant Svan its own DUMP permission. Android audio-session reports stay on-device.

Svan does not use a notification listener to read other apps’ notifications. Its notification permission is used for its own status and foreground-service notifications.

Sharing is your choice

Diagnostics can include phone/build details, player package names, session identifiers, measured levels, and output-device names or addresses. Svan does not automatically upload these logs. Review and redact a report before sharing it. Information posted in a GitHub issue is public.

This website

The Svan website has no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, sign-in or contact form. Fonts and app images are served as site assets. Your sound-feel selection and gallery interactions happen in your browser and are not sent to Svan.

GitHub Pages serves the website. Visiting it involves ordinary hosting requests to GitHub; follow GitHub’s privacy statement for how GitHub handles those requests. Download, source and feedback links take you to GitHub.

More detail

These are beta implementation notes, not a completed Play Store privacy-policy submission. The developer’s full beta privacy document includes publication/contact items still to be finalized before a store release.

No advertising or analytics SDK is present in the app’s reviewed dependency list. Third-party code, data and fonts retain their own licences.

## What this update brings

- Backing vocals and Binaural occupy the first Orchestral amplifier row. They enhance eligible
  stereo detail already in a recording, not separated vocals, identified instruments or added
  binaural-beat tones. The spatial stage preserves the mono sum and shares an energy budget.
  Fast retains its static response when the centre is silent; Detailed also holds pure-side material.
- Bass Resolve and its Svaresa Auto switch bound changes to a bass note's attack/body instead of
  following individual bass cycles. This describes the processor and synthetic checks, not a
  guarantee of listener preference or precise instrument separation.
- Optional Detailed spatial mode, actual latency reporting and aligned clip renders. Fast is
  the default. Detailed adds roughly 21–23 ms; phone performance and listening checks are pending.
  Deadline/underrun recovery can fall back to Fast/Safe 48k without changing saved choices.
- Safe 48 kHz capture remains the default. Opt-in high-rate negotiation uses bounded serial
  fallback, retained source mute across reopen, actual client-format checks, and rate-aware clips,
  WAV headers and rendering. Client capture rates do not prove a streaming source or DAC rate.
  This is not bit-perfect or USB-DAC passthrough. High-rate analysis preserves time windows through
  decimation; its low-band FFT estimates below ~80 Hz remain coarse.
- Read-only “How Svaresa decides” shows the compiled rules, bounds and reasons. It is not a
  trained AI model or live decision audit. Kotlin feature-age/epoch provenance and live skip
  reporting remain pending; the legacy planner entry point retains its evidence defaults.
- Experimental bass unmasking is explicit, off by default and never enabled by Auto. It cuts
  suspected sustained non-note peaks by at most 2 dB; synthetic validation is not music validation.
- Live protection/unmasking metadata follows applied controls; mixed-setting clips are cancelled,
  and source reopens preserve live choices without undoing safety fallback. Large-font labels and
  the Svaresa dock were corrected; per-player capability text distinguishes native processing,
  system-effects approximations, off and unavailable controls.

## Before installing

- Export settings first and retain the backup outside Svan.
- The original owner certificate is unchanged. An owner-signed installation may be eligible for
  an in-place install, but this candidate keeps versionCode 13 and actual phone update behavior
  is unverified. If Android rejects it, stop and report the message; do not force a downgrade.
- Preview installations use another certificate. Export settings, then follow the documented
  preview-to-owner migration (uninstall/install/restore); capture/detection grants may need setup again.
- Keep Play Protect enabled. If Android warns or blocks installation, stop and report it.
- Start with System effects; Audiophile capture is experimental and needs per-session consent.
  Defaults remain Fast, Safe 48k and experimental unmasking off.

Passing these checks is not malware certification, guaranteed sound quality or Play approval.
